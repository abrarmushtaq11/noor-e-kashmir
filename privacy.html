1. Introduction & Core Philosophy

Noor-e-Kashmir ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed as a sacred, distraction-free Islamic reference application providing the Holy Quran with authentic Kashmiri translation (Bayan-ul-Furqan by Mirwaiz Molvi Mohammad Yousuf Shah RA), verified prayer schedules, and daily Azkar.

We believe privacy is a fundamental human right and a religious trust (Amanah). This policy explains clearly and transparently how the App interacts with your device permissions and guarantees that your spiritual journey remains private, respectful, and secure.

2. Device Permissions & Information Usage

A. Location Permission (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

Purpose: Optional. Used solely to determine your geographical coordinates (latitude and longitude) to calculate accurate local Islamic prayer times (Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib, Isha) and magnetic Qibla direction towards the Holy Kaaba.
Processing: 100% On-Device. Your coordinates are processed through internal mathematical algorithms on your phone. Location data is never stored on any server, never shared with third parties, and never tracked in the background. Users can also bypass GPS entirely by manually selecting their district (e.g., Srinagar, Anantnag, Baramulla, etc.) from the district picker.

B. Notifications Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)

Purpose: Used exclusively to deliver local prayer time reminders and Azan notifications at the calculated prayer times. Scheduled locally via Android AlarmManager without communicating with any external notification cloud server.

C. Foreground Service & Audio (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK / WAKE_LOCK)

Purpose: Required under Android 14+ guidelines to allow continuous audio recitation of Surahs and Kashmiri Bayans when your phone screen is locked or when navigating outside the application. Displays standard lock-screen media controls (Play, Pause, Skip Ayah).

3. Advertising & Third-Party Services

Version 1.0.0 of Noor-e-Kashmir is completely Ad-Free.

  • The app does not display banner ads, pop-ups, interstitial ads, or video rewards.
  • The app contains no third-party behavioral analytics, ad tracking pixels, or cross-app tracking identifiers (Advertising ID).
  • Audio recitations streamed or downloaded from verified Islamic public servers (such as EveryAyah or local Kashmiri archive CDNs) are fetched directly over encrypted HTTPS without collecting user metadata.

4. Data Storage & Security

All user preferences (e.g., your last read Ayah, bookmarks, personal notes, selected Kashmir district, and prayer alarm toggles) are stored strictly inside your phone's private app sandbox using an encrypted local SQLite Room database.

Uninstalling the application or clearing app data in Android settings automatically and permanently erases all locally saved preferences. We have no access to your reading history or bookmarks.

5. Children's Privacy

Noor-e-Kashmir is suitable for Muslims of all ages, including children learning Quranic recitation. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. Because our service collects no personal information whatsoever, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.
